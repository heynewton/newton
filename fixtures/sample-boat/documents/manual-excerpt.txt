Section 6: Periodic maintenance (excerpt, pages 41-42)

6.1 General
Do maintenance at the engine-hour interval or the calendar interval,
whichever comes first. An engine used little still needs its yearly
service, because oil and coolant age even when the engine does not run.

6.2 Maintenance schedule

ITEM                                 HOURS      CALENDAR
Engine oil and oil filter            100 h      12 months
Fuel filter element                  200 h      12 months
Raw-water pump impeller              250 h      24 months
Coolant (replace)                    1,000 h    24 months
Valve clearance (check and adjust)   500 h      -
Drive belt (inspect / replace)       250 h      24 months
Zinc anodes in heat exchanger        100 h      6 months
Air intake filter (clean)            100 h      12 months

6.3 Engine oil
Capacity with filter: 4.8 quarts (4.5 liters).
Use oil rated API CH-4 or better, SAE 15W-40 above 5 C.
Check the level every day of use, with the engine stopped and level.

6.4 Raw-water impeller
Close the seacock before opening the pump cover. Replace the impeller if
any vane is cracked, set or missing, and always carry a spare on board.
Running the pump dry for more than 30 seconds damages the impeller.

6.5 Winter lay-up
Change the oil before lay-up, not after, so acidic used oil does not sit
in the engine. Flush the raw-water circuit with non-toxic antifreeze.
